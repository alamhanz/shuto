Commercial or Editorial? A short guide

Commercial photos can be used in ads and on products. So there should be no visible logos or brands, no recognizable artwork or private places without a release, and anyone you can recognize needs a signed model release.

Editorial photos illustrate news, blogs and articles. Logos, brands and people are fine, but the description must follow this form exactly: CITY, COUNTRY - MONTH D, YYYY: one factual sentence (for example LISBON, PORTUGAL - APRIL 3, 2025: Visitors walk along the waterfront promenade.), and the photo must not be edited to change what happened. When unsure, shuto puts the photo under Needs a look.
