What happens to my photos

  • Your original photos are only read. They are never changed or moved.
  • The AI runs on this computer by default, so your photos stay here. If you choose an online AI in Settings, the photos you check are sent to that company.
  • Photos are sent to Shutterstock only when you press the upload button and confirm.
  • You always press the final Submit on the Shutterstock website yourself.

If something goes wrong

The AI helper is not running

Open Ollama from the Start menu (Windows) or Applications (Mac), leave it open, and try again. If it is not installed, see Settings, This computer.

There is not enough space

AI models are large downloads. Free up space on your drive, or pick a smaller model in Settings. If another drive has more room, open Settings, Where to keep AI models and choose a folder there. shuto moves your models for you. On Linux, the "For helpers" box below has the steps.

The Shutterstock login failed

Open Settings, save your email and password again, and press Test login.

The upload failed

Check your internet connection and press Try again. Photos that already went through are not sent twice.

The page says it could not find my folder

Use the Choose folder button on the Add photos page instead of typing the location.

Extra steps

For helpers

On Windows and Mac, keep Ollama's models on another drive from Settings, Where to keep AI models. No commands are needed. On Linux, set the OLLAMA_MODELS setting to a folder on that drive, then restart Ollama. Replace the example folder with your own.

Linux
sudo systemctl edit ollama, then add [Service] and Environment="OLLAMA_MODELS=/mnt/photos/ollama/models", then sudo systemctl restart ollama. The ollama user must be allowed to write to that folder.

To save an online AI key from a terminal instead of Settings: shuto secrets set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (or GEMINI_API_KEY).
