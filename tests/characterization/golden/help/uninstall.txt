usage: shuto uninstall [-h] [--config CONFIG] [-v] [--yes] [--delete-data] [--keep-login]

remove shortcuts and saved login (asks first); optionally shuto's data

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --config CONFIG  path to shuto.yaml
  -v, --verbose
  --yes            do not ask for confirmation
  --delete-data    also delete shuto's database and settings
  --keep-login     keep the saved Shutterstock login
