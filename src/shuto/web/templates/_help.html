Commercial or Editorial? (short guide)

Commercial photos can be used in ads and on products. So: no visible logos or brands, no recognizable artwork or private property without a release, and any recognizable person needs a model release.

Editorial photos illustrate news, blogs and articles. Logos, brands and people are fine, but the caption must follow this form exactly: CITY, COUNTRY - MONTH D, YYYY: one factual sentence (for example KYOTO, JAPAN - APRIL 3, 2025: Visitors walk along the Philosopher's Path.), and the photo must not be manipulated. When unsure, shuto marks the photo for review.
