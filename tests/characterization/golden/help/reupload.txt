usage: shuto reupload [-h] [--config CONFIG] [-v] [--ids IDS [IDS ...]] [--all]
                      [--include-submitted] [--yes]
                      batch

send photos again that you deleted on Shutterstock (needs --yes)

positional arguments:
  batch

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
  --config CONFIG      path to shuto.yaml
  -v, --verbose
  --ids IDS [IDS ...]  photo ids (see `shuto review`)
  --all                every uploaded photo of the batch
  --include-submitted  with --all: also photos noted as submitted
  --yes                confirm they are gone from Shutterstock
