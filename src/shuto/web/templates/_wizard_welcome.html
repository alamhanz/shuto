Welcome to shuto

shuto helps you get your photos ready for a stock photo site. It checks each photo, writes a title, description and keywords, and prepares everything to send.

  • Everything happens on this computer. Your photos are not sent anywhere unless you choose to upload them.
  • Your original photos are never changed.
  • You always press the final Submit button yourself.

Setup takes a few minutes. You can skip any step and come back later in Settings.
